Privacy Policy

Last updated {{ updated }}

Anime Live Countdown (“we”, “us”) is an independent, non-commercial fan project at animelivecountdown.com. This policy explains what we collect when you use the site, why, and what you can do about it. You can browse schedules and countdowns without an account. Most of what follows only applies if you sign in.

What we collect

When you sign in

You sign in with Google, GitHub or Discord. We never see your password for those services. From the service you choose, we receive and store your name, email address and profile picture, and we keep a record of when your account was created and last updated.

What you do on the site

  • The shows you follow and the episode you’ve watched up to
  • Your reactions to episodes
  • Episode reminders you set, and the in-site notifications we send you

Optional features you turn on

  • AniList sync: your AniList user ID and username, an access token that lets us read and update your AniList list (stored encrypted), and when you last synced.
  • Browser notifications: the push address and keys your browser creates for this site. They identify your browser to its push service, not you personally.
  • Discord reminders: the Discord webhook URL you give us.
  • Calendar feed: a private, random token that makes up your calendar link.

Analytics and technical data

We use Google Analytics, with IP anonymization turned on, to see which pages are visited and roughly how people use the site, such as device type, browser and approximate region. Like any website, our servers also receive standard request information, such as your IP address and browser, which may be recorded briefly to keep the service running and secure.

Cookies and local storage

  • Sign-in cookie: when you sign in, we set an HttpOnly cookie that keeps you signed in for up to 7 days. Page scripts can’t read it, and it is only sent to our sign-in endpoints.
  • AniList connection cookie: a short-lived cookie that protects the AniList connect step and is used for nothing else.
  • Analytics cookies: set by Google Analytics.
  • Local storage: your light or dark theme choice is saved in your browser. It never leaves your device.

How we use it

We use this information to run the site: to show your followed shows and progress, send the reminders you ask for, sync with AniList when you connect it, and understand which pages are useful. We don’t sell your information, we don’t show ads, and we don’t use your data to target you.

Who else handles your data

We only share what each service needs to do its job:

  • Supabase: hosts our database.
  • Oracle Cloud: runs our backend servers.
  • Vercel: hosts the website.
  • Google Analytics: receives usage data, as described above.
  • Google, GitHub or Discord: handle sign-in when you choose them. Their own privacy policies apply to your accounts there.
  • AniList: receives your list updates, only if you connect it.
  • Your browser’s push service (for example Google, Mozilla or Apple): delivers reminder notifications, only if you turn them on.
  • Discord: receives reminder messages, only if you add a webhook.

We may also disclose information if the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep your account data for as long as your account exists. You can remove parts of it at any time: unfollow shows, delete reactions and reminders, disconnect AniList (which deletes the stored token), remove your Discord webhook, reset your calendar link, or turn off browser notifications.

Deleting your account

Email {{ email }} from the address on your account and ask us to delete it. We’ll delete your account and everything linked to it (follows, progress, reactions, reminders, notifications and connected services) within 30 days.

Your choices

  • You can use the site without signing in.
  • You can block or clear cookies in your browser. If you block the sign-in cookie, you’ll be signed out more often.
  • You can opt out of Google Analytics with Google’s opt-out browser add-on.
  • Depending on where you live, you may have a right to see, correct or delete your data. Email us and we’ll help.

Security

The site is served over HTTPS, AniList tokens are stored encrypted, and the sign-in cookie can’t be read by page scripts. No service is perfectly secure, but we work to protect what we hold.

Children

The site is not directed at children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect their information. If you believe a child has signed up, email us and we’ll delete the account.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we’ll update the date at the top of this page. If a change is significant, we’ll also point it out on the site.
